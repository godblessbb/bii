Good to know

How long until they arrive?

We aim for 7 to 10 business days door to door inside the US: about a day for our check, a few days to print, then the mail. You get a tracking link by email.

What are they made of?

Thick vinyl with a protective laminate, so they handle water bottles and laptops. Dishwashers and car bumpers are a stretch.

What photos can I use?

Photos you took, or have permission to use. No celebrities, brand logos, cartoon characters or other people’s art. People in the photo should be fine with being on a sticker. No nudity or hateful content.

What if something goes wrong?

If a sticker arrives damaged or doesn’t match the preview you approved, email us a photo within 30 days and we reprint or refund.

What happens to my photo?

We use it only to make your stickers. Unpurchased designs are deleted after 30 days; order files after 180 days.

Do you ship outside the US?

Not yet. US addresses only for now.
