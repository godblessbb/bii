How it works

About two minutes on your side. The drawing happens while you watch.

  1. Upload a photo

    Any phone photo works. We cut out the subject for you, right in your browser.

  2. Pick a style

    Eight looks, from line art to clay figure, or just the photo. Try a few, free.

  3. Choose size and finish

    Die-cut, circle or square. 2 to 4 inches. Glossy or matte.

  4. We print and mail

    Every design gets a human check, then goes to print. Tracking by email.

Good to know

How long until they arrive?

We aim for 7 to 10 business days door to door inside the US: about a day for our check, a few days to print, then the mail. You get a tracking link by email.

What are they made of?

Vinyl stickers made by our print partner, cut to the shape you see in the preview. They are made for laptops, notebooks and water bottles. We don’t promise dishwasher or outdoor durability.

What photos can I use?

Photos you took, or have permission to use. No celebrities, brand logos, cartoon characters or other people’s art. People in the photo should be fine with being on a sticker. Parents can upload photos of their kids. No nudity or hateful content.

What if something goes wrong?

If a sticker arrives damaged or doesn’t match the preview you approved, email us a photo within 30 days and we reprint or refund.

What happens to my photo?

The cutout runs in your browser, so the original photo stays on your device. The cut-out picture is uploaded only to draw your style and print your order. Unpurchased designs are deleted after 30 days; order files after 180 days.

Do you ship outside the US?

Not yet. US addresses only for now.
